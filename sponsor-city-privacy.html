Privacy Policy — Sponsor City

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Sponsor City (com.sponsor.city.epicproject) is a 3D open-world game. This policy explains what the game keeps on your phone, what it sends out when you play with other people, and what it never touches.

It is written to describe what the software actually does. Where something could be read two ways, the stricter reading is the one meant.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in. You never give us an identity.
  • It contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising network. The sponsor brands you see on crates in the city are invented names written into the game.
  • We operate no server. There is nowhere for your data to be sent to us, and we could not read it if you tried.
  • Your progress — coins, history, settings, the campaigns you create — stays in the game's private storage on your phone.
  • Multiplayer sends a little data to public relays run by other people. You can switch it off in Settings.

What the game keeps on your phone

All of it is held in the app's own private storage, which other apps cannot read:

WhatExamples
Player profilean auto-generated player name such as PLAYER_3166, level, crates opened
Game progresscoins, reward history, items bought, shops owned, fines and police record
Campaignsthe sponsor campaigns you create in the game, including any logo image you pick
Settingsgraphics quality, control sensitivity, relay list, multiplayer on or off
Multiplayer keya key pair generated on the device, used to sign what you send. It is never shown to us

You do not type a real name, an email address or a phone number anywhere in the game, and the game never asks for one.

Multiplayer: what leaves your phone

Multiplayer uses the Nostr network. The game connects to public relays — by default nos.lol, relay.damus.io and relay.primal.net — over an encrypted connection (wss://). These relays are run by other people, not by us, and each has its own terms.

While multiplayer is on, the game publishes:

WhatKept by relays?
Your position and movement in the game world, with your player nameNo — sent as ephemeral events that relays pass on and do not store
Chat lines you type or speak into the gameNo — ephemeral in the same way
City events: fines, thefts and other in-game offences, with your player nameYes — these are ordinary events, so a relay may store them and anyone can read them

Two things follow from that, and you should know both before you play online:

  • Anything the game publishes is public. Do not type anything into the in-game chat that you would not put on a public noticeboard.
  • Once an event has reached a public relay, we cannot take it back. We do not run those relays and have no way to delete from them.

Turning it off: open Settings in the game and switch the network to OFFLINE. The whole game still works; you simply play alone. You can also replace the relay list with your own relay.

The position inside the game world is a coordinate in an invented city. The game does not use your device's location, GPS or network location, and asks for no location permission.

Permissions

The game requests one Android permission:

PermissionWhy
INTERNETTo reach the multiplayer relays. Nothing else in the game needs the network

There is no camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location permission. Speech input exists only in desktop browsers that provide it; the Android app has no voice capture at all.

Children

Sponsor City is not directed at children. It is a city game with mild crime-themed play — police, fines and theft — and an open chat with other players, so it is meant for teenagers and adults. It does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Money

Coins in Sponsor City are points inside the game. They are not money, they cannot be bought, and they cannot be cashed out. The game takes no payment and holds no payment details.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Everything the game stores sits on your phone until you delete it.
  • Uninstalling the game erases all of it, including the multiplayer key.
  • Ephemeral multiplayer messages are gone as soon as they are delivered.
  • City events already published to public relays stay with those relays. Playing offline from the start is the only way to publish nothing.

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to make to us.

Changes to this policy

If the game gains a feature that changes any of the above, this policy is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes with it.
